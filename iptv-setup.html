Vanlige spørsmål

How do I set up IPTV for the first time?+
1) Subscribe to NorgesIPTV and receive login details by email. 2) Download an IPTV app (IPTV Smarters Pro, TiviMate or Smart IPTV). 3) Enter Xtream Codes: server URL, username and password. 4) Channels load automatically. Total time: under 5 minutes.
What is Xtream Codes for IPTV setup?+
Xtream Codes is the standard protocol for IPTV connections. You receive a server URL, username and password from NorgesIPTV. Enter these in any IPTV app that supports Xtream Codes for automatic channel loading.
What IPTV app should I use?+
Firestick/Android TV: TiviMate or IPTV Smarters Pro. Samsung TV: Smart IPTV or NET IPTV. LG TV: Nanomid or Smart IPTV. Apple TV/iPhone: iPlayTV or GSE Smart IPTV. Windows PC: IPTV Smarters Pro. All work with NorgesIPTV.
How long does IPTV setup take?+
With NorgesIPTV, IPTV setup takes under 5 minutes. Subscribe, receive login details by email (within 2 minutes), download the app, enter details – done. Norwegian support 24/7 if you need help.
